Scale map: what to play over each chord

        
Pick a bar, pick a scale for its chord, and see it on the neck with the chord’s own notes lit up. Then snap pieces of the scale into your solo like building blocks, bar after bar, and use the “Into the next chord” tips to move from one chord to the next.

Learn to improvise

        
Nine short steps, from three notes to a whole solo. Each one opens in the Studio with a backing and a small task.

        

        
Based on: Berklee Online, Basic Improvisation (lesson order: few notes, call and response, rhythm, motifs, chord tones, approach notes) · Yamaha Music Educators: Off the page (melodies built on chord tones) · Music Teacher: improvisation for beginners (imitation, restrictions) · Elissa Milne: first improvisation games · Premier Guitar: guide tones
